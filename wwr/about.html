Nile Rush Rafting

Our mission is to help families, friends, and explorers experience the power of the river with confidence. Nile Rush Rafting combines trained guides, careful planning, and a love for adventure to create memorable white water journeys.

History

Nile Rush Rafting began with a small group of river guides who believed that adventure should be exciting, safe, and welcoming. Over the years, the company has grown into a trusted rafting team serving visitors who want to discover Uganda's beautiful rivers.
